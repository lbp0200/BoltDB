### SlaveReconnector (`internal/replication/reconnect.go`)
- 新增 `SlaveReconnector` 结构体：状态机（Disconnected→Connecting→Syncing→Connected）
- Exponential backoff：1s → 2s → 4s → … → 60s max，无限重试
